Ukraine strikes the Crimea bridge a second straight day. Zelenskyy calls it an “unprecedented operation”; the road is shut, the rail line damaged.

A drone aimed at a US radar site at Sheikh Isa air base is intercepted. Bahrain reports no casualties.
